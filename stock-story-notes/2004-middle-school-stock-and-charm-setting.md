# 2004 Middle School Stock And Charm Setting

## Core Premise

The protagonist is a third-year middle school student in 2004.

He starts investing with only 100,000 KRW, using future memory to trade Korean stocks. He avoids futures and options because those are too risky and unnatural for a middle school student. The trading style is cash-only spot stock trading.

Alongside future memory, he has a subtle supernatural charm ability. It does not look like magic or mind control. It feels more like people slowly become drawn to him when they meet his eyes and talk to him.

## Account Setup

The protagonist goes with his mother to open a stock account.

Sequence:

1. On Friday, January 2, 2004, he goes to a bank branch with his mother.
2. The mother acts as the legal guardian.
3. They open a minor's securities account in the protagonist's name.
4. A bank-linked securities account is the most natural setup for the time.
5. Kiwoom Securities is the recommended choice.
6. The home laptop is set up with Kiwoom's HTS, Hero Moon.
7. A certificate is issued and installed.
8. The mother technically manages the account and password.
9. The protagonist actually chooses stocks and timing.
10. Trading begins on Monday, January 5, 2004.

Kiwoom Securities fits the 2004 setting because it had a strong online-trading image, low-fee positioning, and the Hero Moon HTS.

## Family Situation

The mother is busy and does not check the account closely.

This lets the protagonist trade quietly without immediate suspicion. At first, the money is small enough that no one around him has a reason to notice.

## Tax And Legal Simplicity

For domestic listed Korean stocks in 2004, an ordinary small shareholder generally does not have to worry about capital gains tax on market-traded listed stocks.

Practical handling:

- Stock transaction tax is withheld automatically when selling.
- Dividend tax is withheld automatically when dividends are paid.
- The protagonist avoids foreign stocks at first to keep tax handling simple.
- Futures, options, margin, and short selling are avoided.

This keeps the story grounded: the protagonist is not trying to exploit complex instruments, only future knowledge and timing.

## Trading Rules

The simulation uses these rules:

- Starting capital: 100,000 KRW.
- Only Korean spot stocks.
- No futures.
- No options.
- No short selling.
- No margin borrowing.
- Buy at the day's opening price.
- Sell at the day's closing price.
- Use as much available cash as possible.
- Shares can only be bought in whole units.
- Leftover cash remains in the account.
- If every trade is unattractive after costs, hold cash.

Cost assumptions:

- Buy commission: 0.025%.
- Sell commission: 0.025%.
- Sell transaction tax: 0.3%.

## January 2004 Result

Trading period:

- January 5, 2004 to January 30, 2004.

Result:

- 100,000 KRW becomes about 286,003 KRW.

This assumes perfect future memory and daily switching into the strongest available stock from the selected Korean stock universe.

Natural story version:

Future-memory version:

- He trades daily.
- He buys the stock he knows will rise that day.

- He avoids losing days by staying in cash.

## Plausibility Of Scale

Quiet growth range:

- 100,000 KRW to a few million KRW: almost no external attention.
- A few million to tens of millions: family suspicion may begin, but securities firms likely do not care.
- Around 100 million KRW: impressive, but still possible to frame as a lucky or talented individual account.
- Several hundred million KRW and above: family, tax, and securities-company attention become more plausible.
- Over 1 billion KRW: the story should include tax/accounting advice and stronger secrecy measures.

For early 2004, ending March with about 2.36 million KRW in the account is still safe and believable.

## Supernatural Charm Ability

The protagonist has a subtle attraction ability.

It is not celebrity-level beauty. He is not a perfect-looking idol type. Instead, he has a strange atmosphere that makes people want to keep looking, talking, and remembering him.

Game-like interpretation:

- While someone maintains eye contact with him, favor stacks slowly increase.
- Conversation increases the speed of stacking.
- Trust, curiosity, or emotional openness makes the effect stronger.
- Fear, disgust, and anger weaken the effect.
- The effect is not instant domination.
- It does not erase free will.
- It lowers guard, creates curiosity, and leaves emotional afterimages.

Possible names:

- Gaze Imprint
- Favor Accumulation
- Lingering Charm
- Eye Contact Bonus
- Bond Stack

Recommended name:

- Favor Accumulation

## Charm Ability Rules

Basic rules:

1. The effect begins through eye contact.
2. The effect grows through conversation.
3. The target feels that the protagonist is easy to talk to.
4. The target does not realize anything supernatural is happening.
5. The feeling remains after the meeting.
6. People who talk to him once may want to talk to him again.
7. Strong hostility cannot be erased immediately.
8. The protagonist can still fail socially if he acts strangely.

This makes the ability quiet and useful rather than overwhelming.

## Story Tone

The protagonist's strongest weapons are:

- Future memory.
- Patience.
- Small starting capital.
- Careful risk avoidance.
- A strange charm that makes people lower their guard.

He does not need to show off. In fact, showing off is dangerous.

The early story should feel like hidden acceleration:

- The money grows quietly.
- The mother remains busy.
- The school sees him as ordinary.
- Friends slowly become more interested in him.
- Adults find him strangely composed.
- The protagonist realizes that money and human attention are both compounding.

## Sample Prose

He did not need a miracle.

One hundred thousand won was enough.

On January 5, he bought Hynix at the opening price and sold it before the closing bell. The profit was not large enough to shock anyone, but it was large enough to prove one thing.

His memory was real.

After that, the rules became simple.

Buy only what would rise.
Sell before greed turned visible.
Stay in cash when the day was wrong.

No futures.
No options.
No borrowed money.

Those were not tools for a middle school student.
They were traps.

What he had was cleaner than that.

He had memory.

And when people looked into his eyes for too long, they remembered him too.
