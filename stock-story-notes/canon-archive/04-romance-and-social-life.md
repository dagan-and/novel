# 04. Romance And Social Life

Updated: 2026-07-20

## General Romance Tone

Yongsik's romance life is powered by:

- His unusual calm.
- His money.
- His intelligence.
- His future-memory confidence.
- His charm/affection-stack ability.

But the tone should be:

- Atmosphere.
- Tension.
- Emotional pull.
- Ambiguity.
- Not crude explicitness.

For adult characters, romantic scenes can use mood, implication, and aftermath rather than graphic detail.

## Charm Ability

Description:

- Eye contact and conversation build affection.
- People remember him.
- Women feel drawn to him, even if he is not a celebrity-level beauty.
- He has the type of atmosphere where a person becomes curious after a short conversation.

Limits:

- It is not mind control.
- It does not remove free will.
- It works better when there is already interest or emotional compatibility.
- It can create opportunities, not guarantee obedience.

## High School Romance

During high school:

- He is always in the top academic group.
- He deliberately avoids ranking first too obviously.
- Girls from:
  - Same class.
  - Academy.
  - Other schools.
  - Friend circles.
  become interested.

Early established examples:

- By March 2004, he had about two girls in a "some" relationship:
  - One from his class.
  - One from academy.

High school tone:

- He is smart, popular, and strangely mature.
- Academies are partly for friends and girls.
- He enjoys the social side because studying is easy for him.

## 2008 January-February Suji Dating

Before Yonsei begins, he spends January and February around Suji.

He has three girls from different high schools so they do not overlap easily:

- Jang Yoonjung:
  - Pungdeok High School graduate.
  - Comfortable local girlfriend/long-known type.

- Kim Gayoung:
  - Suji High School graduate.
  - Bright, lively, social, playful.

- Lee Jaein:
  - Seowon High School graduate.
  - Quiet, emotional, literary/cafe type.

The relationships are best framed as:

- Girlfriend/some boundary.
- Not all publicly official at the same time.
- Each girl senses Yongsik is special and soon leaving for Seoul.
- The mood is "last winter before university."

After March:

- They fade naturally.
- Yongsik does not create dramatic breakup scenes.
- He simply replies late, answers shorter, and stops making time.
- "Out of sight, out of mind" becomes the emotional rule.
- This is cold, but it matches Yongsik's movement into the new Seoul life.

## Weekday Schedule

Final idea:

- Monday: Jang Yoonjung.
- Tuesday: Rest / stocks / driving practice / family.
- Wednesday: Kim Gayoung.
- Thursday: Rest / friends / family / car care.
- Friday: Lee Jaein.
- Saturday: Flexible, one of the three or group/social outing.
- Sunday: Family / solo time / next-week schedule.

Purpose:

- Avoid overlap.
- Give each relationship its own atmosphere.
- Create tension on Saturdays.

## Use Of The Grandeur TG

- Yongsik rents a Grandeur TG.
- It changes his dating radius.
- Girls react differently:
  - Yoonjung: anxious that he is becoming distant.
  - Gayoung: excited and impressed.
  - Jaein: likes the quiet car interior.

The Grandeur is not for folding/lying down.

It is for:

- Warm winter drives.
- Quiet conversations.
- Music.
- Heated seats.
- Fogged windows.
- Suji/Bundang/Seoul movement.

## College Romance

From March 2008:

- Yonsei opens a wider social field.
- Yongsik is in the architecture track, class of 2008.
- He meets:
  - Same department students.
  - Other departments.
  - Academy students.
  - Blind dates.
  - Group dates.
  - Club/nightlife people.

Tone:

- He is not just chasing women.
- He is tasting a youth he never had.

2008 college romance can escalate by semester:

- March-April:
  - Awkwardness, orientation, first drinking gatherings.
  - A 22-year-old third-year architecture senior approaches him first.
  - He is attracted to her older-woman ease and studio-world confidence.
  - He also meets a 20-year-old same-generation girl through a club, keeping the freshman freshness alive.
  - He keeps male classmates at a polite distance.
  - If they ask about his place, he says he lives with a cousin hyung who goes to Hongdae.
  - March dating rhythm:
    - Monday/Thursday: Choi Seo-yoon.
    - Tuesday/Friday: Han Yu-ri.
    - Weekend: whichever of the two is available.
- May-June:
  - Festivals, group dates, first stronger campus crushes.
- July-August:
  - Travel and more adult-feeling dates.
- September-November:
  - More polished dating, better restaurants, stronger gifts.
- December:
  - Enlistment creates farewell tension.

## After Marriage

After Misaki, the bachelor mode ends.

Modern Japan does not officially accept concubine/second-wife culture as normal.

Canon direction:

- Yongsik had his free 20s.
- After marriage, he chooses Misaki.
- He may still face temptation, but the story should use that as a test of commitment, not as a casual second-wife fantasy.

Useful tension:

- Old girlfriends contact him.
- A woman in Japan shows interest.
- Misaki worries.
- Yongsik realizes he is not losing freedom; he is choosing the home he wants to return to.
