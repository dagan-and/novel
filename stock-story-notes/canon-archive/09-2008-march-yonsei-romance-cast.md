# 09. 2008 March Yonsei Romance Cast

Updated: 2026-07-20

This file focuses on the two new women introduced in March 2008 after Yongsik enters Yonsei architecture.

## March Core Mood

March 2008 is not about ordinary male freshman nightlife.

Yongsik already knows what typical 20-year-old male socializing looks like:

- Drinking.
- PC room.
- Billiards.
- Karaoke.
- Cheap late-night meals.

He has experienced enough of that in real-life emotional memory, and it does not feel like the youth he most wants to recover.

What he wants from this alternate life is more specific:

- The tender tension between a 20-year-old man and a woman of the same spring.
- The feeling of being led by a slightly older college woman.
- The awkward but beautiful moment when adult possibilities open for the first time.
- The once-only freshness that cannot be experienced again at 37.

Therefore March centers on:

- Choi Seo-yoon, the 22-year-old architecture senior.
- Han Yu-ri, the 20-year-old club girl.

## Social Boundary

Yongsik is polite with male classmates but keeps distance.

He does not want:

- Men casually asking to come over.
- His three-room becoming a drinking spot.
- His money becoming public through room access.
- Male classmates constantly using his car, room, or wallet.

When asked about his housing, he says:

> "I live with a cousin hyung who goes to Hongdae."

This blocks casual visits without sounding rude.

He still takes architecture seriously:

- Attends lectures with focus.
- Understands drawings quickly.
- Is interested in space, light, plans, and materials.
- Wants to use money later to build places for people he loves.

## Choi Seo-yoon

### Basic

- Name: Choi Seo-yoon.
- Age: 22.
- School: Yonsei University.
- Major: Architecture.
- Year: third-year student.
- Relationship with Yongsik: older department senior who approaches him first.

### Concept

Seo-yoon is the capable older woman of the architecture studio.

She represents:

- College adulthood.
- Late-night studio culture.
- A woman's practiced confidence.
- The thrill of being led rather than always leading.

### Appearance

- Height: about 167 cm.
- Body:
  - Slim.
  - Long arms and legs.
  - Natural B-C cup range.
  - More elegant than cute.
- Face:
  - Calm fox-like impression.
  - Thin double eyelids.
  - Slightly long eye line.
  - Looks composed even when tired.
- Hair:
  - Shoulder-length or slightly longer natural waves.
  - Often clipped up with a hair claw while working.
- Usual style:
  - Black turtleneck.
  - Shirt with sleeves rolled up.
  - Wide pants.
  - Long coat.
  - Thin silver earrings.
  - Coffee in hand.

### Personality

- Relaxed.
- Observant.
- Skilled at handling juniors.
- Teasing but not childish.
- Does not rush.
- Knows men like her, but does not react easily.
- Notices Yongsik is not an ordinary freshman.

### First Hook

During architecture orientation or studio/workroom introduction, she notices that Yongsik understands the explanation too quickly.

She says:

> "You're a freshman, right?"

Yongsik:

> "Yes."

Seo-yoon:

> "Doesn't feel like it. Your eyes aren't surprised enough."

### Dating Mood

Seo-yoon dates are night/studio-based.

Places:

- Architecture studio.
- Late-night Sinchon.
- Quiet izakaya.
- Coffee after midnight.
- Model-material shops.
- His Grandeur.
- His three-room, naturally framed as coffee or discussion.

Typical flow:

- Studio work runs late.
- She asks if he wants to eat.
- They leave together.
- She teases his adult act.
- He tries to stay calm, but she can tell he is still twenty.

Possible line:

> "You're good at pretending to be an adult."

Yongsik:

> "Is it obvious?"

Seo-yoon:

> "A little. That's why it's cute."

### Function

Seo-yoon makes Yongsik feel like a freshman again despite money, future memory, and car.

She gives him:

- Older-woman tension.
- Adult confidence.
- A sense that university is not high school.
- The experience of being pulled into someone else's rhythm.

## Han Yu-ri

### Basic

- Name: Han Yu-ri.
- Age: 20.
- School: Yonsei University.
- Year: freshman.
- Club: photography club.
- Major candidate:
  - Clothing/textile.
  - Psychology.
  - French literature.
  - Life design.
  - Any non-architecture major that keeps her outside his department.

### Concept

Yu-ri is the same-age spring.

She represents:

- Freshman innocence.
- Campus excitement.
- Camera, cafe, and first adult dates.
- The feeling that Yongsik is also only twenty.

### Appearance

- Height: about 160 cm.
- Body:
  - Balanced normal figure.
  - Natural B cup.
  - Soft college-freshman look.
- Face:
  - Between rabbit-like and puppy-like.
  - Round bright eyes.
  - Cheeks rise when she smiles.
- Hair:
  - Shoulder-length bob.
  - Half-up style also suits her.
- Usual style:
  - Light cardigan.
  - Pleated skirt.
  - White sneakers.
  - Small crossbody bag.
  - Film camera or digital camera around her neck.

### Personality

- Curious.
- Bright.
- Talks easily.
- Observes people's faces well.
- Takes photos often.
- Finds Yongsik's adult aura strange and interesting.
- Says things directly because she is the same age.

### First Hook

At a club recruitment booth, Yu-ri is taking photos of new students.

She sees Yongsik passing.

Yu-ri:

> "Wait. Are you interested in photography club?"

Yongsik:

> "I don't know much about photography."

Yu-ri:

> "Then how about being photographed?"

She takes a picture of him smiling. Later, showing him that photo becomes their first private connection.

Yu-ri:

> "This photo is weird."

Yongsik:

> "Why?"

Yu-ri:

> "You don't look twenty."

### Dating Mood

Yu-ri dates are campus/spring-based.

Places:

- Sinchon cafes.
- Yeonhui-dong alleys.
- Hongdae photo exhibitions.
- Ewha dessert shops.
- Club photo walks.
- His three-room only later, at first for "transferring photos" or "choosing pictures."

Tone:

- Not as practiced as Seo-yoon.
- Awkward in a good way.
- She blushes when drinking.
- She asks questions she pretends not to care about.

Possible line:

> "You really live alone?"

Yongsik:

> "Why?"

Yu-ri:

> "No reason. Just... it sounds grown-up."

### Function

Yu-ri keeps Yongsik from becoming too cool too fast.

With her:

- He laughs more naturally.
- He feels his own age.
- His first-year freshness survives.
- The story gets warmth and spring air.

## Weekly Dating Rhythm

Canon March schedule:

- Monday: Choi Seo-yoon.
- Tuesday: Han Yu-ri.
- Wednesday: architecture class, studio, assets, rest.
- Thursday: Choi Seo-yoon.
- Friday: Han Yu-ri.
- Saturday/Sunday: flexible, whichever of the two is available.

Contrast:

- Seo-yoon:
  - Night.
  - Studio.
  - Older woman.
  - Coffee.
  - Adult teasing.
- Yu-ri:
  - Day/spring.
  - Camera.
  - Same-age girl.
  - Cafe.
  - Fresh awkwardness.

## Suji Girls After March

The Suji girls fade naturally.

- Jang Yoonjung.
- Kim Gayoung.
- Lee Jaein.

Yongsik does not make dramatic breakups.

He:

- Replies late.
- Gives shorter answers.
- Says he is busy with school.
- Does not make time.
- Lets distance do the work.

Rule:

> Out of sight, out of mind.

This is cold, but it fits Yongsik's movement into the new Seoul stage.
