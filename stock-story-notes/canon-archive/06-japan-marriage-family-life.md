# 06. Japan Marriage Family Life

Updated: 2026-07-20

## Main Married Base

Final base:

- Kanazawa, Ishikawa Prefecture, Japan.

Earlier options:

- Akita:
  - Good quiet atmosphere.
  - But too inconvenient for Korea travel.
- Sendai:
  - Convenient city.
  - But Pacific-side earthquake/tsunami memory makes it unattractive to future-memory Yongsik.
- Tokyo/Osaka:
  - Too crowded.
  - Too many Koreans/tourists.
  - Less suited to quiet family life.

Why Kanazawa:

- Quiet but not rural.
- Cultural and beautiful.
- Good match for an art-school heroine.
- More realistic Korea access through Komatsu Airport.
- Good place for raising a child.
- Sophisticated without Tokyo/Osaka chaos.

## Korea-Kanazawa Travel

Normal route:

- Korea -> Komatsu Airport -> Kanazawa.

Approximate feel:

- Flight: around 1 hour 45 minutes to 1 hour 50 minutes if direct flights are available.
- Airport movement/waiting: around 40-60 minutes.
- Komatsu Airport to Kanazawa: around 40-55 minutes.
- Total: roughly 3.5 to 4.5 hours.

Story use:

- Yongsik can visit Korea several times per year.
- Not as easy as Tokyo/Osaka, but much easier than Akita.
- Korea visits feel like planned 2-week to 1-month stays, not casual day trips.

- A home where Yongsik returns after Korea work.

Misaki's mother's home:

- Close enough for frequent visits.
- Helps with child care.

## Korean Parents' House

Yongsik builds or buys a countryside-style two-story detached house.

Structure:

- First floor:
  - Parents' living space.
- Second floor:
  - Yongsik/Misaki/child private area.
  - Small living room.
  - Bedroom.
  - Bathroom.
  - Small kitchen.

Meaning:

- Not traditional in-law living.
- More like a family villa/private floor.
- Misaki can feel safe because privacy exists.
- Parents can see the grandchild often during visits.

Why this is good:

- If Yongsik stays in a hotel or separate apartment, parents see the child less.
- If they share one house without privacy, Misaki may feel burdened.
- A separate second floor solves both.

## In-Laws

Misaki's parents:

- Initially may worry:
  - Korean husband.
  - Age gap.
  - Unusual job.
  - Wealth.
- They come to like him because:
  - He lives near them.
  - He respects their daughter.
  - He supports Misaki comfortably.
  - He helps with house repairs, health checks, family travel, car, and child-related expenses.

Support should feel warm, not like buying approval.

Good support examples:

- House renovation.
- Health checks/dental/medical expenses.
- Family trips.
- Comfortable car.
- Small atelier/shop for Misaki and her mother if desired.

## Marriage Tone

The marriage is not "money buys happiness."

The deeper point:

- Money removes pressure.
- Misaki creates home.
- Yongsik stops wandering because he finally has a place he wants to return to.
