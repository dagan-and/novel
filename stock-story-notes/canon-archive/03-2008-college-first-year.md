# 03. 2008 College First Year

Updated: 2026-07-20

## Position Of 2008 In The Story

2008 is the first major adult freedom year.

Yongsik is:

- Born in 1989.
- Entering Yonsei University.
- Already financially unusual.
- Not yet a mega-rich public-level person.
- Free enough to live very differently from ordinary students.
- Still careful enough not to spend hundreds of millions openly.

This year should feel like:

- First car.
- First adult dating radius.
- First Seoul lifestyle.
- First taste of living like a young rich man.
- Last year before KATUSA service.

## Starting Capital In January 2008

Approximate starting capital from previous projection:

- Total assets by end of 2007: about 309 million KRW.
- Parents' housing / Suji jeonse support: about 150 million KRW.
- Mother's side dish shop or support: about 50 million KRW.
- Stock operation capital for 2008: about 100 million KRW.
- Emergency/ready cash: about 10 million KRW.

The practical feeling:

- He is rich for a college freshman.
- But much of his capital is allocated.
- He should not spend like someone who already has 100 billion KRW.

## Monthly Spending Capacity

Recommended monthly spending feel:

- January-February:
  - 3-5 million KRW per month for ordinary life and dating.
  - Around 10 million KRW in a heavy month if car setup/initial costs are included.
- March-May:
  - Around 4-5 million KRW per month.
  - Rich student feeling, but not absurd.
- June-August:
  - Around 7-10 million KRW per month.
  - Summer travel, car, better dates, stronger adult freedom.
- September-November:
  - Around 10-15 million KRW per month possible.
  - He now looks like a student from a very wealthy home.
- December:
  - Spending is reduced and organized.
  - KATUSA entry and SM investment preparation.

Average for 2008:

- About 7 million KRW per month is the best story balance.
- Low months: 3 million KRW.
- High months: 15 million KRW.

## Car Setup

Final chosen car:

- Hyundai Grandeur TG.
- Long-term rental, not purchase.
- Start: January 2008.
- End: around December 2008 before military service.

Reason:

- He will enter KATUSA late 2008, so buying a car is inefficient.
- Grandeur TG is natural for 2008 Korea.
- It is less flashy than BMW/Mercedes but still impressive to 20-year-old students.
- In Suji/Bundang, it can look like a parent's car.
- At Yonsei, it makes him look like a well-off student.

Estimated cost:

- Monthly rental: 900,000-1,100,000 KRW.
- Deposit / initial fee: 3-5 million KRW.
- Fuel: 300,000-600,000 KRW per month.
- Parking/tolls: 100,000-300,000 KRW per month.
- Cleaning/miscellaneous: 50,000-100,000 KRW per month.
- Total monthly car cost: about 1.35-2.1 million KRW.

January setup cost:

- Initial rental cost/deposit: about 5 million KRW.
- January car operation: about 1.7 million KRW.
- Dating/life: about 3 million KRW.
- Total January spend: about 10 million KRW.

Public explanation:

> "I'm going to the military in December, so I rented it only until then."

Or:

> "It's under my mom's contract, and I use it before enlistment."

## Why Not A Van Or Folding Car

Earlier candidates:

- Chrysler Grand Voyager.
- Hyundai Veracruz.
- Kia Mohave.
- Kia Carnival.

They are better for folding seats or car-camping style space.

But the final Grandeur TG is chosen because:

- Better 2008 college image.
- More natural in Suji, Bundang, and Yonsei.
- Less strange for a 20-year-old than a large minivan.
- Better for winter date atmosphere.

The rear seats are not meant for full flat sleeping.

The car is used for:

- Warm winter drives.
- Quiet conversations.
- Cafe-to-cafe movement.
- Bundang/Suji/Seoul dating.
- The feeling that Yongsik has suddenly become an adult.

## January-February Before Yonsei

Setting:

- Still based in Suji.
- Meets girls from different high schools.
- Drives around Suji, Bundang, Jukjeon, Bojeong-dong, Gangnam.

Mood:

- Last winter before Seoul.
- First adult car.
- Girls feel he has changed.
- Yongsik is excited but already half-looking toward Yonsei.
