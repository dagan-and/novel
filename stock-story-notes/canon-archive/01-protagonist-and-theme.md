# 01. Protagonist And Theme

Updated: 2026-07-20

## Basic Profile

- Name: Kim Yongsik.
- Birth year: 1989.
- Nationality: Korean.
- Main genre frame:
  - Future-memory wish fulfillment.
  - Stock/crypto wealth-building.
  - Youth recovery.
  - Romance and eventual quiet family life in Japan.

## Core Abilities

Yongsik has three central advantages.

1. Future memory.
   - He knows important stock, entertainment, crypto, and real estate trends.
   - He uses this first to escape ordinary financial pressure.
   - Later he deliberately stops using it aggressively because too much wealth becomes socially noisy.

2. Extraordinary intelligence.
   - Practical effect is like an IQ above 160.
   - He can remember books after reading them once.
   - Mathematical answers and problem solutions often appear in his head immediately.
   - In school he can rank first, but he hides his full ability.

3. Charm / affection stack.
   - When he makes eye contact and talks, people's favor slowly stacks up.
   - It is like a game mechanic where affection rises through eye contact and conversation.
   - It is not direct mind control.
   - It appears as atmosphere, pull, warmth, and memorable presence.
   - Women often feel he is strangely comfortable and hard to forget.

## Personality

- Does not like excessive attention.
- Enjoys freedom but is not trying to become publicly famous.
- Can be calculating with money, but emotionally wants warmth and family.
- After wealth is achieved, he wants to be quiet rather than become a celebrity rich person.
- Gives generously to family and relatives, but prefers legal/tax-clean methods.
- His life principle after 2014:
  - Do not hide money.
  - Pay taxes.
  - Keep records.
  - Live as an explainable quiet rich person.

## Emotional Core

The story is not only about becoming rich.

The emotional truth is that Yongsik wants to recover what poverty and survival pressure stole.

In the user's real-life emotional background:

- He spent college years fighting for scholarships.
- He studied hard to rank 1st or 2nd in his department.
- He worked parking jobs, Chinese restaurant serving jobs, mascot jobs, and other exhausting side work.
- Time was always split and squeezed.

The fantasy therefore means:

- Not childish greed.
- Not just money worship.
- A wish to live the youth that was taken by pressure.

## Large Life Arc

### Teens

Yongsik uses future memory to overturn his life.

- Starts with 100,000 KRW in 2004.
- Learns to control attention.
- Builds the first meaningful capital base.
- Keeps grades high but not too visibly perfect.
- Charm ability begins creating romantic possibilities.

### 20s

He recovers lost youth.

- Enters Yonsei University in 2008.
- Enjoys dating, cars, academies, travel, clubs, cafes, and social life.
- Serves in KATUSA while SM investment grows.
- Uses Bitcoin to reach final financial freedom.
- From 2014, he spends, travels, supports family, and lives freely.

### 30s

He chooses home.

- Meets Sakurai Misaki in 2022 in Kanazawa.
- Marries her in 2024.
- Builds a Japanese family base.
- Still visits Korea often.
- Becomes a husband and father rather than a wandering bachelor.

### 40s And Later

He is a quiet rich man.

- A warm husband.
- A good father.
- A son who lets his parents live comfortably.
- A man with enough money to stop chasing money.

## Core Sentence

Yongsik does not earn money to dominate the world. He earns money to recover the time and happiness that poverty once took from him.

Misaki is the person he meets at the end of that wandering freedom.

She becomes the home he finally wants to return to.
