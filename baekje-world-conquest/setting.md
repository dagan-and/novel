# Setting

## Core Premise

Baekje has already unified Mahan. At this point, an immortal transcendent with modern knowledge appears and begins transferring practical knowledge into the state. The ultimate goal is not regional dominance but phased world conquest.

## Strategic Identity

- Base state: Unified Baekje-Mahan
- Hidden advantage: immortal leader with long-horizon planning
- Real constraint: modern knowledge exists, but industrial infrastructure does not
- Immediate objective: convert knowledge into stable production, administration, and military superiority

## Early Reform Priorities

1. Standardize iron tools and expand agricultural productivity.
2. Build reservoirs, irrigation, storage depots, and road links.
3. Reform army organization into consistent command units.
4. Introduce hygiene, quarantine, and cleaner water practices.
5. Standardize records, tax rolls, and logistics accounting.
6. Improve coastal shipping, ship design, and navigation.

## Tone

The campaign should feel like a long-form strategic chronicle. Victories should come with political strain, supply pressure, and resistance from both elites and conquered populations.
