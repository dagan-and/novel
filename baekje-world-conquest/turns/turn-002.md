# Turn 002

## Strategic Posture

Cheonin launches the Iron and Water Program, arguing that one extra harvest is worth more than one reckless victory.

## Major Actions

- reservoirs and irrigation ditches are expanded under army supervision
- blacksmith guilds are reorganized into licensed state suppliers
- military workshops begin producing standardized spearheads, arrowheads, and farming blades
- boiling water and quarantine orders are enforced in major settlements

## Outcomes

Crop forecasts rise sharply after spring planting. Disease in the barracks declines, which shocks conservative officers who had viewed illness as fate. The first true advantage appears: Baekje can now keep more men alive, more often, for less cost.

## State Changes

- agricultural output rises
- barracks health improves
- iron production becomes more centralized
- local cult hostility increases in some districts

## Risks Next Turn

- forced labor demands may trigger peasant fatigue
- priestly factions may frame reforms as impiety
