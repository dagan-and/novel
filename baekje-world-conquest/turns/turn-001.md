# Turn 001

## Strategic Posture

Cheonin rejects an immediate invasion and declares the First Rectification: no world conquest is possible without a state that can count grain, move iron, and punish corruption faster than its enemies can mobilize.

## Major Actions

- a land survey begins across the Baekje-Mahan heartland
- iron tool standards are imposed on state workshops
- village granaries are inventoried and sealed under royal marks
- a courier road program links river ports, arsenals, and provincial seats

## Outcomes

Harvest loss from theft and local hoarding falls for the first time. Aristocrats protest the survey, but common farmers quietly support it because tax demands become more predictable. The army is not larger yet, but it is beginning to eat on schedule.

## State Changes

- food security improves
- administrative visibility increases
- elite resentment rises
- campaign readiness begins to build

## Risks Next Turn

- nobles may turn reform resistance into open conspiracy
- early success may tempt premature foreign war
