# Turn 007

## Strategic Posture

With the peninsula secured and Wa aligned, Cheonin starts the Arsenal Decade early. The aim is not one more army, but a state that can replace losses, outbuild fortresses, and campaign every year.

## Major Actions

- state arsenals expand iron casting, bow production, and armor repair
- salt, timber, and key mines are brought under stricter crown management
- account ledgers track output, loss, and corruption by district
- river transport is reorganized into scheduled military convoys

## Outcomes

Baekje becomes visibly different from its neighbors. Its power no longer depends on one gifted general or one lucky harvest. It is becoming a machine that converts order into force. Smuggling and graft still exist, but now they can be measured, and therefore punished.

## State Changes

- military resupply becomes dependable
- corruption detection improves
- crown revenue rises
- merchant classes gain influence through state contracts

## Risks Next Turn

- over-centralization may choke local initiative
- new merchant wealth could create a rival power bloc
