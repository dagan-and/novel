# Turn 008

## Strategic Posture

The Northern Test begins. Rather than launch a grand invasion, Cheonin orders a limited Liaodong operation to seize one corridor, one port, and one proof that continental expansion is practical.

## Major Actions

- spies bribe frontier intermediaries and identify underpaid garrisons
- a mixed land-sea strike targets a coastal approach tied to inland roads
- engineers raise prefabricated earth-and-timber fortifications on captured ground
- proclamation tablets offer protection and lower taxes to towns that switch allegiance quickly

## Outcomes

The operation succeeds with alarming efficiency. Frontier defenders fight hard, but Baekje’s logistics decide the matter: wounded men are removed faster, arrows arrive faster, and commanders know their rations more precisely than the enemy knows its own numbers. A small but vital bridgehead is established in Liaodong.

## State Changes

- first continental foothold secured
- frontier credibility rises
- supply doctrine proves exportable
- hostile powers begin treating Baekje as an imperial threat

## Risks Next Turn

- larger continental states may intervene before Baekje is ready
- occupying foreign land could expose administrative limits
