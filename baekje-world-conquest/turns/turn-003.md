# Turn 003

## Strategic Posture

With the core economy stabilizing, Cheonin begins military reform without declaring war. The goal is to create an army that marches like one body instead of a feud of local warbands.

## Major Actions

- the army is divided into permanent units of 10, 100, and 1000
- depot officers are separated from battlefield commanders
- written drill instructions are copied and distributed
- coastal shipwrights are gathered for a royal ship design conference

## Outcomes

Training becomes harsher but far more efficient. For the first time, replacement troops can join existing formations without collapsing unit cohesion. The navy remains small, yet its hulls begin to carry more grain and men with less loss at sea.

## State Changes

- command structure becomes more reliable
- mobilization speed improves
- naval transport capacity increases slightly
- aristocratic private retinues lose relevance

## Risks Next Turn

- displaced noble captains may join anti-court plots
- rigid drills could lower morale if not balanced with rewards
