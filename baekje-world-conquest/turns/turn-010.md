# Turn 010

## Strategic Posture

At the close of the tenth turn, Cheonin unveils the Doctrine of the Four Horizons: secure the peninsula, command the sea, fortify Liaodong, and prepare the first great continental war.

## Major Actions

- a full imperial census is completed across core territories
- elite academies are founded to train administrators, engineers, physicians, and staff officers
- permanent naval stations are built to bind Wa to Baekje trade and force
- Liaodong defenses are strengthened while diplomats probe rival courts for fractures

## Outcomes

Ten turns in, Baekje has not yet conquered the world, but it has become the kind of state that makes world conquest thinkable. Its armies are disciplined, its granaries counted, its roads useful, its fleets feared, and its enemies forced to confront a terrifying fact: they are not merely behind in strength, but behind in statecraft itself.

## State Changes

- Baekje emerges as the dominant organized power of its region
- the empire possesses a stable rear, a maritime sphere, and a continental foothold
- institutions begin surviving beyond any one campaign season
- world conquest shifts from fantasy to long-term program

## Risks Next Turn

- a grand coalition could form against Baekje
- Cheonin’s immortality may create succession and legitimacy distortions
- expansion into the deeper continent will cost far more blood than the first decade
